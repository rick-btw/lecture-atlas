Eigenvalues and Diagonalization

Diagonalization reveals the coordinate system in which a linear transformation acts by independent scaling.

Definition. A nonzero vector \(v\) is an eigenvector of \(A\) if \(Av=\lambda v\) for some scalar \(\lambda\).

\[ \det(A-\lambda I)=0 \]

Lemma. Eigenvectors corresponding to distinct eigenvalues are linearly independent.

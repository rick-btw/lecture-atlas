Linear First-Order Equations

The integrating factor method turns a non-exact expression into a single derivative.

Standard form. \( y' + p(x)y = q(x) \)

\[ \mu(x)=e^{\int p(x)\,dx}, \qquad y(x)=\frac{1}{\mu(x)}\left(\int \mu(x)q(x)\,dx+C\right). \]

Warning. Normalize the equation before computing the integrating factor.
